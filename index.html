Each project answers one business question, tests its own numbers and ends in a recommendation. Code, tests and full reports are on GitHub.

People analytics in Python: pandas, scikit-learn, survival analysis, Streamlit

Who resigns, and can a risk list find them in time?

Resignations cluster in the first two years and in one department, which loses 2.5 % of staff a year against 0.5 % elsewhere. A gradient boosting model tested on unseen years ranks leavers with ROC AUC 0.93.

  • Ten years of HR records for 6,284 employees, with retirements, layoffs and resignations treated as separate processes
  • Discrete-time survival analysis and models validated on later years, not a random split
  • Fairness audit: a simple tenure rule finds 100 % of the men who resign but only 44 % of the women
  • Streamlit explorer for cohort survival, resignation rates by group and the fairness of a risk list, at group level only

Marketing experiment in R: tidyverse, ggplot2, rstanarm, R Markdown, Shiny

Should the shop switch to a one-page checkout?

Yes on mobile, test again on desktop. Conversion rose by 0.51 points (95 % interval 0.27 to 0.75), almost all of it from mobile, worth about €45,000 a month in the simulation.

  • Power analysis before the data, sample ratio check, and average order value as a guardrail
  • Frequentist and Bayesian estimates, with segment effects from a Bayesian logistic regression
  • Simulation showing that checking results daily raises false winners from 5 % to 22 %
  • Shiny app to plan the test, read a simulated run against the known true effect, and see the cost of peeking

Customer analytics warehouse in PostgreSQL, with Python for loading and charts

Which customers come back, and which ones carry the revenue?

19 % of customers order again in the month after their first order and 11 % are still active after a year. The best customer segment is 24 % of customers and 54 % of revenue.

  • Raw exports staged and checked with 12 data-quality rules before a star schema is loaded
  • Reporting views for monthly KPIs, cohort retention, RFM segments and product ranking
  • Ten SQL tests that reconcile rows and revenue, enforce one row per order and stop the build on any mismatch

Marketing machine learning in Python: pandas, scikit-learn, SciPy, Streamlit

Which customers will not order again in the next 90 days?

The simplest full model wins: logistic regression reaches ROC AUC 0.83 on a later period, 0.034 above a recency-only baseline. 97 % of the highest-risk tenth churn, against 17 % of the lowest.

  • Leakage-safe snapshots: features are built only from data available on the snapshot date
  • Hypothesis tests with Holm correction and K-Means segments before any model is fitted
  • Streamlit decision simulator: choose the contact threshold under stated cost assumptions and see expected against observed value
  • A tested, installable Python package with 31 tests and continuous integration

How I work

AI tools now write much of the routine code and reporting. What stays valuable is knowing which question matters, whether the data can be trusted and whether a result holds up.

Business question first

Every analysis starts from a decision someone has to make and ends in a report that leads with the findings, not the method.

Data quality before models

Validation checks, reconciliation tests and documented cleaning rules come before any chart or model.

AI output gets audited

I use AI coding assistants and language models, and treat their output as a draft: tests, out-of-sample evaluation and fairness audits decide what stays.
